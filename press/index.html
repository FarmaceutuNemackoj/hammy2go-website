Short description

Hammy2go is a cheeky hamster card game for 1 to 5 players on one phone. Who hoards wisely, eats at the right moment and cleans up before the store overflows? No ads, no in-app purchases, fully offline.

Full description

In Hammy2go every player is a hamster with a store of their own. Taking turns, you grab cards from the table and eat them for points – which also produces droppings that clog up your store. Hoard too greedily and your store overflows.

On top of that there are 23 bonus cards, from the dropping catapult to the fridge, combos for berries, nuts, grains and herbs – and predators: the cat snatches the fish, the bird takes the worm. Two to five players share one phone and simply pass it around, or you play solo against two computer opponents.

A Daily Challenge with the same cards for everyone, seasonal cards, over 75 achievements and unlockable gifts (hamster looks, tables, holiday cards, music) keep it fresh. Hammy2go works offline, collects no data and is available in 10 languages.
