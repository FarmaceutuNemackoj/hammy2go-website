Questions & answers

What does Hammy2go cost?

You buy it once (2.99 € in Germany, prices vary by country). Everything is included – no ads, no in-app purchases.

Which devices can I play on?

Hammy2go is coming soon for iPhone (App Store) and in November for Android (Google Play).

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Hammy2go works completely offline and collects no data. Your progress stays on your device.

How many players?

1 to 5: play solo against the AI, or 2 to 5 on one device – humans and AI can be mixed.

Which languages?

Deutsch, English, Srpski, Français, Español, Italiano, 日本語, Русский, Türkçe, 한국어. The game starts in your phone’s language.

Can real hamsters eat all of this?

No! Hammy is a cartoon hamster. Real hamsters must not have chocolate, alcohol, sweets, salty food or citrus fruit – if in doubt, ask your vet.

How can I give feedback?

We’d love to hear from you: info@hammy2go.com
